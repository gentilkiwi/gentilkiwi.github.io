You need 4 connectors by board: 1 x `SSQ-106-03-*-S`, 1 x `SSQ-110-03-*-S` & 2 x `SSQ-108-03-*-S`. The 1 x `SSQ-103-03-*-D` (SPI / Programming connector is optional)
